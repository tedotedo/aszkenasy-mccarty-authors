About the authors

Two doctors who write for children facing medical procedures, and for parents and professionals who need clear information.

Both are very experienced doctors. Their children have autism too. That shapes the books — what they choose to explain, and how plainly they say it.

Dr Odet Mark Aszkenasy

Consultant paediatrician

Mark is a consultant paediatrician at Care ADHD. Until September 2026 he was an NHS Consultant Community Paediatrician and Clinical Lead for autism diagnostics.

He writes the children’s All About My Health series, and the parent and professional guides on autism genetics and leucovorin.

Dr Moira McCarty

Consultant Paediatric Radiologist

Moira is a Consultant Paediatric Radiologist. She wrote Anna’s Ultrasound Adventure to help children understand a tummy ultrasound before they come to hospital.

The paperback is co-authored with Mark. There is also a free companion app for families preparing for the scan.
