# Design notes — Aszkenasy & McCarty preview

Quiet clinic site, not a shouty shop. Soft cream paper, warm brown accents, dark ink for reading.

## Palette

- Page: `#faf7f2` cream
- Cards: white on `#e4d9c8` borders
- Ink: `#2c2a26` / soft `#5a564e`
- Accent: `#8b6b4a` (links, labels) with wash `#efe6d8`
- Footer: near-black ink with cream text

Contrast kept high enough for body text; accent used sparingly for meta labels and links.

## Typography

- Display: **Fraunces** (Google Fonts) — soft serif for wordmark, headings, book titles
- Body: **Source Sans 3** — calm, readable UI/body stack with system fallbacks
- British English throughout (`en-GB`)

## Layout

- Mobile-first; max content width ~68rem
- Sticky header with wordmark + three text nav links (Home / Books / About)
- Home: soft radial wash behind hero; featured book grid; quiet “Coming soon” strip
- Books: three groups with short clinic-English notes; coming-soon cards muted (no Amazon CTA)
- About: shared note first (experienced doctors; children with autism; that shapes the books), then two equal bio cards
- Book cards: cover (2:3) + title + one-line blurb + Kindle/Paperback/companion links
- Footer: Associates disclosure + optional PAR link

## Voice

Plain clinic English. Short concrete sentences. No soft metaphors. Credentials shown as **Dr Odet Mark Aszkenasy** and **Dr Moira McCarty**.
